Cave Diving

A generated flooded cave, one guideline, and a third of your gas for the way in.

Dive options
How to play
  • Follow the amber guideline in, reach the end of the line in the far chamber, and get back to the entrance pool before your gas runs out.
  • You start with 200 bar. Turn for home when a third is gone: the gauge marks the turn pressure. Every cave is checked so that turning there from the main line leaves enough gas to get out, even feeling your way along the line blind.
  • Kicking hard, bumping rock and squeezing through tight spots stir up silt. It hangs in the water for a long time, so the mess you make on the way in is waiting for you on the way out.
  • Hold Space near the line to keep a hand on it. In zero visibility it is the only reliable way out, and the arrows on it, which you can feel, point toward the exit.
  • Side passages end in unsurveyed leads, worth points if you get home. Some have their own line across a gap. You carry one 30 m spool (R) to bridge a gap or to leave a line behind you.

WASD / arrows: swim · Shift: kick hard · Space (hold): hold the line · R: spool · M: map · H: controls · Esc: pause
