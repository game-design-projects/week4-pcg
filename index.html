Cave Diving

A generated flooded maze, a reel of line, and a third of your gas for the way in.

Dive options
How to play
  • Find your way through a generated flooded maze to the end chamber, then get back to the entrance pool before your gas runs out. The first dive is a tutorial.
  • No one has laid a line for you. Tie your reel in at the post in the pool (R) and it pays out line as you swim. Swim back over it and it reels back in, so dead ends don't leave a tangle. Press R again to tie it off.
  • You start with 200 bar. Turn for home when a third is gone: the gauge marks the turn pressure. If your line runs unbroken from the pool and you turn there, you always have the gas to follow it out, even blind.
  • Kicking hard, bumping rock and squeezing through tight spots stir up silt. It hangs in the water for a long time, so the mess you make on the way in is waiting for you on the way out.
  • Hold Space near a line to keep a hand on it. In zero visibility it is the only reliable way out. The arrow shows which way it runs back.

WASD / arrows: swim · Shift: kick hard · R: tie the reel in / off · Space (hold): follow a line · M: map · H: controls · Esc: pause
